Since 2023, I am a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the Humanoids and Human Centered Mechatronics laboratory, led by Dr. Nikos Tsagarakis, where I have been leading a commercial project for building two bi-manual quadrupedal prototypes. Together with the managing aspects, which involved me in the coordination of the work and communication, I led the software development team for the codesign and the control framework of the robot, both for the execution of locomotion and manipulation tasks.

I joined the Humanoid and Human Centered Mechatronics Laboratory (HHCM) at the Italian Institute of Technology (IIT) on November 2019 as a Ph.D student  defending my thesis on offline and online planning and control strategies for the autonomous locomotion of humanoid and legged robots in 2023.

I received the bachelor and master degree (cum Laude) in Mechanical Engineering from Politecnico di Milano in 2017 and 2019 respectively.

My research interests span from classical and optimal control of heavily redundant floating-base robots, such as humanoids and quadrupeds, to model-based and model-free learning algorithm for the autonomous execution of loco-manipulation tasks.
Recently, I am also diving into foundation models to enable these robots to scene understanding and action reasoning, depending on the perceived environment and context.
